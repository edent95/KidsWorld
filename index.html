About us · 关于我们

像家一样的幼儿园
A kindergarten that feels like home

小乐儿园地在亚依淡(Ayer Itam)陪伴孩子已经超过 20 年。我们为 3–6 岁的孩子提供全面的幼儿成长教育,由受过培训的老师照顾。每个孩子都被看见、被听见。我们相信孩子最好的学习方式是玩——动手做、一起唱、到户外跑,在好奇心里学会语言、数字和与人相处。

For over 20 years, Kids World in Ayer Itam has offered holistic early childhood development for ages 3–6, with trained caregivers who make sure every child is seen and heard. Our caring teachers turn curiosity into confidence through play, stories, art and lots of laughter.
